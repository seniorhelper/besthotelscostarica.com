About Best Hotels Costa Rica

Updated November 10, 2026

Costa Rica doesn't have one best hotel. A cloud-forest lodge in Monteverde, a surf hotel in Santa Teresa and a big beach resort in Guanacaste are different trips. This guide is built around that: it helps you choose a region and a style of stay first, then shortlist places that fit.

Who publishes it

Best Hotels Costa Rica is published by Eye To Ad Media, a small independent publisher and marketing company in Denver, Colorado, owned by Zach Wennstedt. Eye To Ad Media runs a handful of travel guides covering Central America, the Caribbean and Hawaiʻi.

We're not a tour operator or a travel agency, so we can't book, quote or change reservations.

How we put the guide together

  • By region, then by style. Arenal, Monteverde, Manuel Antonio, Guanacaste, the Nicoya Peninsula, the Osa Peninsula and the Caribbean coast each get an honest summary of what staying there is like.
  • Sustainability claims are checked against the official program. When we talk about the national CST sustainability certification, we explain what it measures rather than treating every "eco" label as equal.
  • No fake reviews or star scores. We don't invent ratings or guest quotes.
  • Live prices, not stale ones. Room rates change by season, so we point you to a live search instead of printing numbers.

How it's funded

The guide is free. If you book through the Expedia search on our pages, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. We may show ads, and businesses can buy labeled featured listings. None of that changes how we describe regions or hotels. See the affiliate disclosure.

Corrections

Lodges change owners, rebrand and close for rainy-season maintenance. If something here is wrong, tell us and include a link to the property's own site.
