Privacy and How This Site Earns Money

Updated November 10, 2026

This policy applies to besthotelscostarica.com, an independent guide to eco-lodges, beach resorts and boutique hotels across Costa Rica's regions. It explains what we collect, which outside companies may set cookies, and where our income comes from.

What we collect

When you just read the site: our host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security. We also use Google Analytics 4, which uses cookies to tell us how many people visit, which regions and guides they read, the type of device and the rough area they're in. We use those totals to decide what to write next, not to identify anyone.

When you send us a form: the listing and advertising form asks for details such as your name, email, phone and business. That message goes to our publisher Eye To Ad Media's lead server (clients.eyetoad.com) and its inbox, and we use it only to answer you. We don't sell or rent it.

The Expedia hotel search (affiliate disclosure)

The hotel search on our pages comes from Expedia Group's affiliate program. Using it, or clicking through to Expedia, lets Expedia and its tracking partners store a cookie that credits this site for the visit. If you book a stay, we may be paid a commission by Expedia. Your price is the same either way. Searching, booking and payment all happen on Expedia under its own privacy statement; we never see your card details or itinerary.

We write about regions and types of stay because they suit different trips, not because of commissions, and we can't make or change bookings.

Ads

Pages may carry ads from Google (AdSense). Google and other vendors use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits here and to other sites, and Google's ad cookies let it and its partners do so across the web. Turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings or opt out of other vendors at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses data from partner sites. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you'll be asked to consent before personalized ads load.

Hotels and businesses can pay for a featured listing. Those are labeled, and their links are marked sponsored.

Choices and requests

Your browser lets you refuse or clear cookies; the guide still works, though the hotel search may not. To see or delete a message you sent us, ask through the contact page.

Children and changes

We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.
